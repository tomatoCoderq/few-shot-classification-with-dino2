\section{Question, Data and Methods}
How do frozen DINOv2 representations compare with supervised ResNet-50 transfer
learning when only $K=1,5,10,20$ labeled examples per breed are available?
Oxford-IIIT Pet has 7,349 images of 37 breeds. The official test split contains
3,669 images. From trainval we reserve 740 validation images (20 per breed,
seed 2026), leaving a disjoint pool of 2,940. Five nested support sets per $K$
(seeds 0--4) are shared by all methods. Hyperparameters and epochs use validation;
test is excluded from fitting and selection. Input is resized to 256, center-cropped
to $224\times224$, and normalized with ImageNet statistics.

Frozen ResNet-50 (ImageNet-1k V2), DINOv2 ViT-S/14 and ViT-B/14 provide L2-normalized
2,048-, 384- and 768-dimensional features. Logistic regression (LR) selects
$C\in\{0.1,1,10,100,1000\}$ on validation. Prototype uses cosine distance to class
means; cosine k-NN uses inverse-distance votes and validation-selected
$k\in\{1,3,5,10,20\}$, $k\leq K$. E5/E6 fine-tune the last ResNet block/all layers:
30 epochs, AdamW, batch 32, head/backbone learning rates $10^{-3}/10^{-4}$,
weight decay $10^{-4}$, fixed BatchNorm, random crops/flips, best validation epoch.

\section{Comparison and Ablations}
With LR fixed, DINOv2-S trails ResNet at $K=1$ (73.4 vs.\ 75.8), matches it at
$K=5$, and improves by 2.1 percentage points (pp) at $K=20$. The paired $K=20$
gain is $2.1\pm0.4$ pp across identical seeds. DINOv2-B + LR is the strongest
observed method at every $K$, reaching $94.2\pm0.3$ accuracy and $94.2\pm0.3$
macro-F1 at $K=20$; its paired gain over ResNet + LR is $3.3\pm0.4$ pp.

Changing only the classifier on DINOv2-S at $K=20$ gives LR 93.0, prototype 91.6
and k-NN 89.9. Increasing S to B with prototype fixed adds 5.2 pp at $K=1$
and 1.3 pp at $K=20$. Frozen ResNet + LR outperforms both fine-tuning variants at
all $K$. This suggests that adaptation is difficult under the fixed training
budget; overfitting and under-optimization cannot be separated by these results.

\section{Limitations, Reproducibility and Contributions}
Validation adds 740 labels beyond the support budget. Architecture/pretraining data
differ; gains cannot be attributed solely to self-supervision. Pretraining overlap
was not audited. Standard deviations describe support sampling; failures concern one seed.

Python 3.12, PyTorch 2.14.1, torchvision 0.29.1, scikit-learn 1.9.1; README gives
the locked environment and local-venv commands for \texttt{src.evaluate},
\texttt{src.analyze} and \texttt{src.reporting}. CSVs, curves and provenance are included.
Restored E7b seed-0 accuracy is 94.36\% vs.\ 94.41\% recorded: two fewer correct
(0.055 pp), logged separately. Tables retain original metrics.\\
